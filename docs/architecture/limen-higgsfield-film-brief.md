# LIMEN — brief cinematografico per Higgsfield

Stato: integrazione Higgsfield installata, verificata il 20 ottobre 2026. In questa chat non sono ancora disponibili strumenti Higgsfield per catalogo modelli, invio delle generazioni o verifica dei lavori. Nessuna nuova clip inviata o generata. Sessione web autenticata precedentemente verificata in Safari; l’utente ha scelto di usare l’integrazione.

## Formato e direzione

Film di circa 45 secondi, orizzontale **16:9** per la landing page, master previsto 1920 × 1080, 24 fps. Generare clip separate e montarle in un unico filmato. Comporre le scene direttamente in orizzontale, senza deformazioni né bande nere.

Realismo cinematografico: persone adulte credibili, pelle e mani naturali, studio contemporaneo, pietra scura, legno, metallo e luce dell'alba. Antracite, avorio e oro tenue. Movimenti fisici di camera, profondità di campo naturale, grana delicata. Stesso protagonista, abiti e ambiente lungo tutto il film. Animazione intesa come movimento realistico di persone, oggetti e camera.

Voce italiana adulta, calma e autorevole, senza enfasi da televendita. Musica strumentale discreta con pulsazione lenta; suoni reali di carta, respiro e tastiera. Scritte e marchio da comporre nel montaggio, per garantirne la leggibilità. Eventuali schermate del prodotto richiedono catture reali: le inquadrature generate mostrano soltanto monitor fuori fuoco.

## Prompt visivo comune (inglese)

Create a premium photorealistic cinematic brand film for LIMEN, an Italian trading education and disciplined decision-making platform. Landscape 16:9 composition for a website landing page, target 1920x1080 at 24fps. The story follows one consistent adult protagonist in a believable contemporary study at dawn, moving from distraction toward deliberate preparation, risk planning, scenario observation, pre-action checklist, practice, journaling and review. Show real people and tangible materials, restrained natural acting, physically plausible movements, realistic hands, skin pores, cloth and reflections. Charcoal stone, dark wood, ivory paper, muted brass accents, soft window light. Slow dolly moves, subtle lateral tracking, natural lens perspective, shallow but credible depth of field, subtle film grain. Compose every shot for a landscape 16:9 final frame, preserving the face, hands and important objects. Keep screens defocused; do not invent readable software UI, chart labels, logos or typography. Reserve clean space for text added in editing. End on a real doorway filled with soft daylight, then a clean dark brand end card added in post. The film should introduce both purpose and process: education, risk awareness, preparation, practice, journaling and review, followed by the free introduction, application, admission and paid Protocol access. Never imply guaranteed financial performance.

Negative direction: no cartoon, anime, illustration, stylized 3D, game-engine look, plastic skin, fantasy architecture, magical particles, holograms, floating charts, surreal transitions, exaggerated wealth, luxury cars, money rain, profit claims, distorted anatomy, extra fingers, fake readable UI, generated captions or generated brand lettering.

Generate each shot separately where required by model duration limits; maintain a common character reference and visual treatment. Assemble and mix the final film afterward. Validate actual clip dimensions and final 16:9 export before delivery.

## Generazione delle clip, una alla volta

Usare il prompt comune insieme al prompt della singola clip. Generare la prima clip, controllarla e ricavare un fotogramma di riferimento per quelle successive, se il modello lo supporta. Non avviare un lotto di varianti casuali: prima verificare realismo e continuità. Nessun dialogo generato nelle clip; narrazione unica in montaggio. Le durate richieste al generatore devono essere almeno pari a quelle del montaggio.

1. **clip-01.mp4 — minimo 6 s, richiesta 8 s.** Landscape 16:9, photorealistic live-action opening shot. A 35-year-old man with short dark hair, light stubble and a charcoal linen shirt sits at a dark oak desk in a contemporary stone-walled study at dawn. A phone vibrates beside an ivory notebook. He calmly turns it face down, then looks toward the soft window light. One slow lateral dolly, natural breathing, restrained expression. Background monitors completely defocused. No text, no speech, no cartoon styling.
2. **clip-02.mp4 — minimo 7 s, richiesta 8 s.** Same man, wardrobe, oak desk and dawn lighting as the reference. Medium side view, he opens the ivory notebook and carefully prepares his workspace. Subtle push-in, warm window highlights and charcoal shadows. Realistic documentary acting. Keep notebook writing unreadable. No generated titles or speech.
3. **clip-03.mp4 — minimo 8 s, richiesta 8 s.** Same study and character reference. Photorealistic close-up sequence within one continuous gentle camera move: hand places a pencil beside a paper checklist, pauses over a planning notebook, then calmly checks a single box. Screen with a market chart remains distant and out of focus. Show deliberate risk preparation through physical actions. Correct hand anatomy, no floating graphics or readable generated UI.
4. **clip-04.mp4 — minimo 8 s, richiesta 8 s.** Same man and study. He moves his hand away from the mouse, waits thoughtfully while observing the blurred monitor, then writes a short entry in the notebook. Slow over-the-shoulder dolly, natural motions. An ordinary practice and review session, no profit celebration. No generated text or speech.
5. **clip-05.mp4 — minimo 11 s, richiesta 12 s.** Photorealistic educational scene in the same dark oak and warm daylight visual world. Same protagonist studies a physical book beside a laptop whose screen is defocused. Cut naturally to an adult teacher at the same desk reviewing a paper exercise with him; teacher points to the paper, protagonist listens and makes a note. Restrained professional interaction, credible faces and hands. Leave the lower frame uncluttered for access-path labels added in post. No readable generated text, no speech.
6. **clip-06.mp4 — minimo 5 s, richiesta 6–8 s.** Same man, charcoal linen shirt, same stone study. Wide horizontal composition. He closes the notebook, stands naturally and walks toward an open doorway filled with soft morning daylight. Slow backward dolly, real architecture, gentle highlights, no fantasy glow. End with a calm composition suitable for a separate LIMEN end card in editing. No generated logos or letters.

Salvare i file in una cartella dedicata. Il comando `python3 scripts/assemble-limen-higgsfield.py /percorso/clip` prepara un montaggio visivo Full HD di 45 secondi; se presente, usa `narration.wav`. Titoli, marchio, musica e controllo finale restano passaggi successivi. Non sostituire il video attuale della landing prima della verifica del nuovo master.
