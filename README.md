## 🛠️ The 6 Sovereign Pillars of AUREUS OS

### 1. Il Banco di Forgiatura (The Forge of the Past)
- **High-Fidelity Market Replay**: Replay historical Forex, Gold (XAUUSD), Indices, and Crypto candle-by-candle with configurable speed and real tick precision.
- **Systematic Edge Calibration**: Execute simulated positions with exact stop-loss, take-profit, spread, and commission parameters to measure true mathematical expectancy.
- **Zero Retrospective Bias**: Hide future price action until you actively step the market forward.

### 2. Il Libro della Verità (The Book of Truth)
- **Automated MT4/MT5 Broker Synchronization**: Direct read-only connection via FX Blue protocol—no manual CSV editing and no exposure of master trading credentials.
- **Mathematical Telemetry**: Instant calculation of R-Multiple distribution, Maximum Adverse Excursion (MAE), Maximum Favorable Excursion (MFE), Profit Factor, Sharpe Ratio, and Drawdown Duration.
- **SHA-256 Notarial Verification**: Every trade record and performance report receives an immutable cryptographic hash, generating a publicly verifiable certificate of integrity.

### 3. Il Custode della Soglia (The Guardian of the Threshold)
- **Pre-Trade Gatekeeper**: Enforces an objective checklist before any trade can be placed (HTF narrative, liquidity sweep confirmation, RR >= 1:2 verification).
- **Position Size & Risk Calculator**: Calculates exact lot sizing based on account balance and maximum defined risk percentage (e.g. 0.5% or 1%).
- **Hard Anti-Tilt Circuit Breaker**: Detects rapid consecutive losses, emotional revenge trading, or lot size escalation, locking the operator out of the terminal.

### 4. Le Prove del Fuoco (The Ordeals of Fire)
- **Multiplayer 1v1 Disciplinary Arenas**: Challenge peer operators to blind historical chart duels governed by strict execution rules.
- **Integrity Hierarchy**: Operators are ranked not by monetary volume, but by adherence to risk parameters, discipline indices, and loss containment.

### 5. L'Ordine Giornaliero (The Daily Order)
- **Morning & Evening Protocol**: Step-by-step psychological calibration, neural breathing timers, and macro economic calendar awareness before market open.
- **Bio-Emotional Telemetry**: Correlates operator emotional states (calm, hyperactive, fatigued) with historical P&L to identify emotional leakages.

### 6. Architettura Patrimoniale (Wealth Architecture)
- **The 5 Sovereign Pillars of Wealth**: Holistic asset management integrating liquid trading capital, real estate reserves, sovereign liquidity, physical precious metals, and cash flow assets.
- **1,000-Trajectory Monte Carlo Engine**: Projects 5-to-10 year capital compounding pathways under various variance, win rate, and drawdown regimes.
- **Prop Firm Survival Matrix**: Models maximum trailing and daily drawdown limits against risk-per-trade variables.

## 💻 Tech Stack & Architecture

- **Frontend**: React 19, TypeScript, Vite 7, Tailwind CSS, Radix UI, Lightweight Charts, Wouter.
- **Backend API**: Node.js, Express 5, TypeScript, WebSockets (ws), Redis Distributed Locks.
- **Database**: PostgreSQL with Drizzle ORM.
- **Spec-Driven**: OpenAPI 3.1 specification (`lib/api-spec/openapi.yaml`) with Orval type-safe React Query hooks.
- **Security & Integrity**: WebCrypto SHA-256 trade signatures, Cloudflare Turnstile, Clerk Authentication, Stripe Billing.
